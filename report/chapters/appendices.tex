\chapter{Reproducibility}\label{app:repro}

The code, the settings, the results summaries, the frozen pre-registration, its deviation log
and this report are kept in one private repository, available from the authors on request. The
EEG data are public: they are downloaded from figshare~\cite{wang2026figshare}, where Wang et
al.\ published them under the CC~BY~4.0 licence. Table~\ref{tab:repro-commands} lists the
steps, run from the top folder of the repository; its runbook (\code{docs/RUNBOOK.md}) says
what each one should print.

Four things keep the numbers the same on a re-run: \textbf{seeds} fixed in the settings files
(Appendix~\ref{app:hyper}); \textbf{the window cache's manifest} (Section~\ref{sec:prep});
\textbf{every result row}, which stores the git commit
that made it and hashes of its settings and of the data manifest; and \textbf{git tags} at fixed
points: \code{setup-v1}, \code{prereg-v1} and \code{prereg-rq5-v1} (plans frozen before the main
results) and \code{results-v1}; \code{demo-v1} and \code{submission-v1} will follow. Classical methods give identical numbers on a re-run; networks trained on a GPU
(Section~\ref{sec:tools}) can differ slightly.

\section{Changes after the plan was frozen}

deviation log with its date and reason, and with what results were known at the time. The log
has \nDeviations{} dated entries; Table~\ref{tab:app-deviations}
lists every one, in our own words. Most clarify a rule before any calibration result. Before the confirmatory results the
networks were re-run, after we found that they had skipped the last incomplete batch of each
epoch. After them, the planned electrode-count study was dropped, a sanity check whose premise
proved wrong was recorded as failed, and the analyses marked post hoc were added. The full text
of each entry is in the deviation log, available from the authors on request. No change altered
a confirmatory outcome.
