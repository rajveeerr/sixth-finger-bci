\section{Dataset and Integrity Audit}
\label{sec:dataset}
We used the dataset of Wang et al.~\cite{wang2026dataset,wang2026figshare}: EEG of 24
stroke patients (18 male, aged 34--77 years; 15 with right and 9 with left hemiplegia)
recorded before a two-week training programme, immediately after it, and one month
later. In each 14\,s trial the patient rested for 7\,s, saw a preparation cue for 1\,s,
and then imagined for 6\,s either grasping with the affected hand (AH) or flexing a
robotic sixth finger worn on the affected wrist (SF). Each session comprised four runs
of 20 trials (10 AH, 10 SF in random order). EEG was recorded at 250\,Hz; the
authors' preprocessing (5--35\,Hz band-pass, common average reference, manual artifact
rejection and ICA) yielded 40 analysis channels~\cite{wang2026dataset}. We used these
preprocessed files.
