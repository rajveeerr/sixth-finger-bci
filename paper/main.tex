\section*{Data and Code Availability}
{\raggedright
The EEG data are public: Wang et al.~\cite{wang2026dataset}, figshare~\cite{wang2026figshare},
CC~BY~4.0. The code, the configurations, the frozen pre-registration and the
deviation log are available from the authors on request; the supplementary
material lists every setting and command. The demonstrator:
\texttt{sixthfinger.rajveers.com}.\par}

\section*{Ethics Statement}
This study is a secondary analysis of publicly released, de-identified data.
Tianjin Huanhu Hospital's Medical Ethics Committee gave the original study its
approval (IEC-B-003-V3.0) and participants consented to data sharing~\cite{wang2026dataset}.
No new data were collected.
