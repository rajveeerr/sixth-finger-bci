\section*{Acknowledgment}
We are grateful to Wang et al.\ for publishing their recordings and analysis code; without

\section*{Funding and Conflicts of Interest}
This work received no external funding. The authors declare no conflicts of interest.

\section*{Ethics Statement}
No new data were recorded for this work, which re-analyses public, de-identified
recordings. Tianjin Huanhu Hospital's Medical Ethics Committee approved the original
study (IEC-B-003-V3.0), and its participants agreed to the sharing of their
data~\cite{wang2026dataset}.

\section*{Data and Code Availability}
{\raggedright
The EEG recordings are public under CC~BY~4.0: Wang et al.~\cite{wang2026dataset}, deposited
on figshare~\cite{wang2026figshare}. The code, the settings files, the frozen
pre-registration and its deviation log are kept in a private repository and are available
from the authors on request, with the supplementary material of the eight-page version of
this paper, which lists every setting and command. The demonstrator runs at \texttt{sixth-finger-bci.vercel.app}.\par}
